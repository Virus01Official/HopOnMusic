Earn money from your music on HopOn Music. Learn how to get started and maximize your earnings.

How It Works

Upload Your Music

Upload your original music tracks to HopOn Music. Make sure your tracks are high quality and properly tagged with title, artist name, and genre.

Get Streams

Share your music with the HopOn Music community. The more people listen to your tracks, the more potential earnings you generate.

Earn Revenue

Earn money from streams. Your earnings are calculated monthly and paid out via your preferred payment method.

Track Analytics

Monitor your streams, listener demographics, and earnings in real-time with our detailed analytics dashboard.

Payouts

Payout Details
  • Minimum Payout: $100 (or accumulated balance carried over to next month)
  • Payout Schedule: Monthly, issued on the 15th of each month
  • Payment Methods: PayPal, Direct Bank Transfer, and Stripe
  • Currency: All payouts in USD
  • Reporting: Full earnings statement available in your dashboard

Monetization Requirements

Basic Requirements
  • Account must be at least 30 days old
  • Minimum 1,000 total streams
  • Minimum 100 followers
  • Verified email address
  • Original music or proper licensing
Rights & Compliance
  • You own or have rights to all uploaded content
  • No copyright infringement or plagiarism
  • Comply with all platform terms & conditions
  • No Artificial Inteligence has been used to create the song
  • No artificially inflated streams
  • Regular account activity required

Tips to Maximize Earnings

Use high-quality cover art, write compelling song descriptions, and include relevant genre tags. This helps your music get discovered and recommended to more listeners.

Share your HopOn Music links on Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, and other platforms. Encourage your followers to listen and add your tracks to playlists.

Engage with the community, collaborate with other artists, and create playlists. Building genuine connections increases listener loyalty and repeat plays.

Upload regularly and maintain high production standards. Consistency keeps your audience engaged and helps build a loyal listener base.

Check your analytics regularly to see which songs perform best, where your listeners are from, and what times they listen. Use these insights to improve your strategy.
